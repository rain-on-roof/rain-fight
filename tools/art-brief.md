You are producing illustration assets for RAIN FIGHT, a browser fighting game by the indie studio Rain on Roof. Use your image generation tool. Save every final image as PNG into the folder `assets/art/` of this repository (create it). Do not modify any other files.

## Art direction (applies to every image)

- Cartoon fighting-game character art, badass but playful. Clean vector look.
- Thick dark plum outlines (#2b2131), flat cel shading with one shadow tone, strong neon rim light: cyan (#4ff3ff) from the left, hot magenta (#ff4fd8) from the right.
- Night, heavy rain, neon city mood. Palette: deep indigo/purple night, neon cyan and magenta accents.
- Original characters only. No logos, no text, no watermark, no resemblance to any existing franchise (do NOT look like Mortal Kombat, Street Fighter or any known character).
- All four characters must look like they belong to the same game: same line weight, same shading, same lighting.
